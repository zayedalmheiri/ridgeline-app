Ridgeline privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026. Ridgeline is a hiking app published by Zayed Almuhairi (developer name PLUFF).

Ridgeline records your hikes on curated trails, counts the trails you complete and lets you take on small challenges with friends. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a one-time code. There are no passwords.
  • Your display name and home region, which you choose.
  • Your hikes: the GPS points recorded while you record a hike (position, time, accuracy, speed and altitude reported by your phone), any gaps in recording, and the results we compute from them: distance, climb, XP, trails completed, badges and challenge credits.
  • Your challenges: the friend challenges you create or join and the invites you send.
  • Anonymous product analytics: a few app events (for example "hike finished" or "collection progress") with a random ID that is not linked to your account. They never contain location, routes, exact times or challenge details.

Location

Ridgeline uses your precise location only while you are recording a hike, including when the screen is locked or you switch apps, so the hike is not cut short. Android shows a notification the whole time it records. Ridgeline does not track your location when you are not recording, and there is no live location sharing.

Why we use it

  • To record your hike, check it against the curated trail and award XP, trail completions and badges.
  • To run the friend challenges you join.
  • To understand, in aggregate and without identifying you, which parts of the app work.

We do not sell your data, show ads or use your data for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Your hikes, tracks and profile are private: visible only to you.
  • In a friend challenge, the other members see your display name, your count (for example 2 of 4) and whether each credit is provisional or counted. Never your tracks, times or which routes.
  • Share cards are drawn from the curated route line, not your GPS track, and contain no date or time. Nothing leaves your phone until you tap Share.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores your account and hikes (database and sign-in).
  • OpenFreeMap serves the map background. Like any website, it sees your phone's internet address and the map area on screen. It never sees your GPS track.
  • Email delivery: sign-in codes are sent by email through our email provider.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting a hike removes its GPS points immediately and reverses its XP, trail completions, challenge credits and badges. Deleting your account removes all of your data immediately. Supabase keeps encrypted backups that expire on their own schedule; we never restore deleted data from them.

Your choices

  • Export: Me → Export my data gives you a GPX and JSON copy of everything stored about you.
  • Delete your account: in the app, Me → Delete account. Or see how to delete your account, including without the app.
  • You can stop location access at any time in your phone's settings; recording will stop working.

Children

Ridgeline is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
