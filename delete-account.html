Delete your Ridgeline account

Ridgeline is a hiking app by Zayed Almuhairi (developer name PLUFF).

In the app

  1. Open Ridgeline and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email zayed1516@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Ridgeline account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Delete some data, keep your account

To delete a single hike: open Ridgeline, go to the Me tab, open the hike and tap Delete hike. Its GPS track is deleted immediately, and its XP, trail completions, challenge credits and badges are reversed. Your account and other hikes stay.

To have other data deleted without closing your account, email zayed1516@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and say what to delete. We do it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted when you delete your account

Everything: your profile, email sign-in, hikes and GPS tracks, XP, trail completions, badges, challenge memberships, challenge credits and invites. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Our database provider keeps encrypted backups that expire on their own schedule; deleted data is never restored from them. Anonymous analytics events are not linked to your account and are kept in aggregate.

Want a copy first? In the app, Me → Export my data saves a GPX and JSON file of everything stored about you.
